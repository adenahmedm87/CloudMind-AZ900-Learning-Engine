PLP Hackathon • Education Technology

CloudMind.

The Learning Intelligence Engine

An independent AZ-900 learning companion that helps learners understand mistakes—and decide what to study next.

Project by Aden Ahmed Mohamed

01 • The problem

Practice scores don't tell the whole story.

Learners can get questions wrong without knowing why, or guess correctly while still misunderstanding the concept. Generic revision may not address the real learning gap.

Unclear mistakes

Wrong answers need useful explanations.

Hidden uncertainty

A correct guess can hide a misconception.

Unfocused revision

Learners need a practical next step.

02 • Our solution

Make every answer useful.

CloudMind combines practice results with self-reported confidence to highlight possible misconceptions and guide targeted review.

Practice

Topic questions and mock exams.

Understand

Explanations and transfer questions reinforce concepts.

Improve

Review priorities and next-step recommendations.

03 • How it works

From attempt to action.

  1. Answer a practice question and report confidence.
  2. Review the result and explanation.
  3. Identify uncertain answers and possible misconceptions.
  4. Follow targeted recommendations and track progress.
04 • Who it helps

Built for independent learners.

CloudMind supports learners preparing for Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900), especially those who need a clear, structured way to practise, reflect, and revise.

Accessible practice

Self-paced learning support.

Actionable feedback

Move beyond a score to a next step.

Progress awareness

Keep revision focused on learning needs.
